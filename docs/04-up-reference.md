## ⚠️ The big correction: UPSCFDC runs TWO different things

We had been treating UPSCFDC as simply "the NSFDC State Channelizing Agency for UP." That is only half of it.

| Track | What it is | Money type | Our earlier coverage |
|---|---|---|---|
| **NSFDC channel credit** | Term Loan, Micro Credit, Education Loan etc., funded by NSFDC at 5% to the SCA | **Loan** | ✅ Covered in Doc 1 |
| **PM-AJAY Grant-in-Aid** | Central scheme, cluster/group enterprise setup with a capital **subsidy** plus a bank loan | **Grant + bank loan** | ❌ **We completely missed this** |

**Why it matters:** PM-AJAY Grant-in-Aid has *no income ceiling*, runs on a completely separate portal, uses a different committee, and gives a straight ₹50,000 subsidy. A citizen walking into a UPSCFDC district office could be eligible for either, both, or neither, and **nothing currently tells them which.** That is our scheme-matching problem, live, inside a single agency.

Worth one teammate installing AJAY-Udyami and screenshotting it, same trip as the PM-SURAJ registration.

## PART B: PM-AJAY Grant-in-Aid, the full process

### B1. What the beneficiary gets
- **₹50,000 per person, OR 50% of project cost, whichever is lower**, as grant.
- **Beneficiary contributes 5%** of project cost.
- **Remainder is a bank loan**, from any bank the beneficiary chooses.
- **CGTMSE guarantee cover fee is borne by the beneficiary.**
- Free skill training on top, under the Skill Development Mission quality framework.

### B2. Eligibility (note how different this is from NSFDC credit)

| # | Criterion |
|---|---|
| 1 | Must be Scheduled Caste |
| 2 | Must be resident of the district applied in |
| 3 | **Age 18 to 50 years** (NSFDC credit has no upper age limit) |
| 4 | **Must be literate to the extent the project requires** (साक्षर होना अनिवार्य) |
| 5 | Must be willing to work as part of a group/cluster |
| 6 | Not a defaulter on any corporation scheme, and must not have settled a prior loan via OTS |
| 7 | Excluded if a legal heir inherited a prior corporation-funded asset (shop, laundry, vehicle) and is still in default |
| 8 | **NO income ceiling at all.** Priority to families with annual income ≤ **₹2.50 lakh** |
| 9 | The project must not already be financed under any other state/SCA umbrella scheme |
| 10 | **Family is the unit** for group/cluster purposes |

**Priority groups:** women, divyangjan (persons with disabilities), and — notable and UP-specific — **applications from the transgender community resident in UP**.

**Earmarks:** 15% of total grant reserved for SC women's income-generating schemes. Minimum 30% women participation in skill-development programmes.

### B3. Group and cluster rules

**Group (समूह)** — acts as a single borrowing unit, joint liability:
- Minimum **3** members, maximum **10** (beyond 10 needs MD approval)
- Elected President, Secretary, Treasurer. Treasurer must have financial knowledge
- Each member gets equal grant: 50% of equal per-member project cost, or ₹50,000, whichever is lower
- **Must meet at least once every 3 months**, minutes recorded in a register, report filed to district office
- At least 2 authorised representatives handle bank transactions and P&L
- **Representatives rotate every 6 months**
- Member exit requires equal-liability replacement, approved by the District Social Welfare Officer

**Cluster (क्लस्टर)** — individual loans, shared activity:
- Each member borrows separately and is **individually liable**
- Different bank branches may finance different members
- Loan sized per member's individual absorption capacity, even for identical activities
- **All cluster members must join the AJAY-Udyami app** for forward linkage
- Bank guarantee under **CGTMSE is mandatory** for cluster projects

### B4. The Pre-Scrutiny Committee (this is the real decision-maker)

| Role | Position |
|---|---|
| **Chairman** | District Social Welfare Officer (Development) / District Manager, Anugam |
| **Member Secretary** | Assistant Manager / Assistant Project Manager |
| Member | Tally Operator (Accounts) |
| Member | Senior Village / Assistant Development Officer |

- Scrutiny happens **2 days per week**.
- Scrutiny includes: **CIBIL score verification**, **penny-drop bank account validation**, document check, and a recovery-certificate check confirming the applicant is not an existing defaulter.
- Committee members **cannot** apply for their own family members in their own district.
- District-level projects then need **DLPAC** approval (chaired by the District Magistrate). State-level projects need Chief Development Officer sign-off.

### B5. Bank stage and rejection handling
- Bank must accept or reject within **5 days** of receiving the application.
- On rejection, the branch manager must return it **with written justification** on the portal/file to the District Social Welfare Officer.
- **A rejected application can only go back to a bank after the District-level Supervision, Evaluation and Monitoring Committee re-approves it.**
- Vendors must be registered on the **e-RUPI / GeM** platform; payment goes **directly to the vendor**, never to the beneficiary.
- Livestock projects are exempt from the vendor-registration rule, but animals must be purchased and ear-tagged within **15 days** of loan sanction.

### B6. Subsidy Reserve Fund, the 18-month lock
- Grant is transferred to the bank and parked in a **Subsidy Reserve Fund (SRF)** linked to the beneficiary's loan account.
- Held for **18 months**. Unit visit report required every **6 months**.
- After 18 months (or earlier if the beneficiary repays on time), it is credited to the loan account.
- If the unit is not operating at the first 6-month review, the beneficiary gets **1 month** to start; failing that, **the grant is recovered**.
- Separately: if the beneficiary has not started within **3 months** of sanction, after **2 reminders**, recovery proceedings begin.

### B7. The District-level Supervision, Evaluation and Monitoring Committee

| Role | Position |
|---|---|
| **Chairman** | Chief Development Officer |
| Member | Deputy Commissioner, Self-Employment |
| Member | Lead District Manager |
| **Member Secretary** | District Social Welfare Officer (Development) |
| Member | Assistant Manager / Assistant Project Manager |
| Member | Senior Village / Assistant Development Officer |

The CDO may co-opt other officers or **SC social workers** as special invitees.

### B8. Training structure

| Type | Duration | Timeframe |
|---|---|---|
| Short Term Training | 120–600 hours | 2–4 months |
| RPL Training | 32–80 hours | 1 month |
| EDP Training | 80 hours | 10–15 days |
| RPL-based Training | 80 hours | 10–15 days |

**Training partner payment:** 50% on beneficiary upload + batch approval → 30% on 75% attendance with passed assessment and certificate → 20% after unit established or 6 months post-placement verification.
**Rates:** Category-I ₹49/hr, Category-II ₹42/hr, Category-III ₹35.10/hr.
**Batch size:** 40–50 beneficiaries. **70% of a batch must be placed or self-established.**
Training centres need Aadhaar biometric/facial attendance, NVR-based CCTV with live feed, and Google location verified on the portal by the district office.

### B9. Reporting cadence
- Districts file a **monthly progress report to HQ**. (The covering letter says by the **2nd** of each month; the SOP body says by the **5th**. Note the inconsistency, use the 2nd.)
- Beneficiary lists are forwarded to **MLAs and MPs**, to the state government via the CDO, and uploaded to the **Government of India portal**.
- District office does hand-holding for **18 months** after financing.

## PART D: Application form fields and document checklist

### D1. Form प्रपत्र-01 (grant sanction application) — exact fields
1. Beneficiary number (from selection letter)
2. Beneficiary name
3. Father's / husband's name
4. **Aadhaar number** (12 boxes)
5. Age (years / months / days)
6. Address + PIN
7. **Mobile number** (must be Aadhaar-linked and active)
8. Number of units/animals in the established unit
9. Address of unit location + PIN
10. Itemised expenditure table on unit establishment
11. Total cost (figures and words)
12. **Bank details: account number, branch name, IFSC code, branch address + PIN**
13. Declaration + signature
14. Passport-size self-attested colour photo

### D2. Form प्रपत्र-02 (applicant's own form, dairy variant) — additional fields
- Educational qualification
- **Land available to applicant/family (in acres)**
- **Cultivable land available for green fodder (acres)**
- Current livestock count, broken down: cow / buffalo / calf (m) / calf (f) / bullock
- Breed of cows
- Current total milk produced (kg)
- Current market available for the milk
- Current surplus/saleable milk (kg)
- Is applicant a member of a milk society? (Y/N)
- Has applicant received dairy-related training? (Y/N)
- **Distance to nearest veterinary hospital (km)**

**Design insight:** these are exactly the questions a voice-first intake should ask, and most are answerable by speaking a number. This is a ready-made script for our Bhashini-powered intake flow.

### D3. Mandatory attachments (generic set)
- Aadhaar card, self-attested
- Caste certificate, **issued by a competent authority and verified online**
- Income certificate, **verified online**
- Residence certificate
- Bank passbook copy / cancelled cheque
- Copy of the selection letter
- Notarised affidavit committing to maintain the asset for at least 3 years (early sale triggers grant recovery by the District Executive Committee)

**Livestock projects add:** purchase receipt, transit insurance record, transport cost receipt, 3-year livestock insurance record, chaff-cutter purchase record, shed construction expenditure record, microchip/ear-tag certificate and ID number, vet health certificate naming the breed.

### D4. Project report must compute
1. Total project cost
2. Beneficiary contribution (5%)
3. PM-AJAY grant amount
4. Training amount
5. CGTMSE cover fee (borne by beneficiary)

**This is our EMI/cost calculator spec, verbatim from a government SOP.**

## PART H: What this unlocks for the build

1. **Scheme matching has a real dataset.** 16 projects, fixed costs, fixed skill codes, explicit gender restrictions. We can build and demo the matcher on genuine government data, not invented schemes.
2. **The cost calculator spec is handed to us.** Total cost → 5% beneficiary contribution → grant → training → CGTMSE fee. Straight from the SOP.
3. **The voice intake script is handed to us.** प्रपत्र-02's fields are mostly spoken numbers: land in acres, animals owned, milk produced, distance to vet hospital.
4. **Partner routing has real gate conditions.** CIBIL check, penny-drop, no-default certificate, 5-day bank SLA, rejection requires committee re-approval. These are concrete rules, not hand-waving.
5. **We know exactly who the officer-side user is**, down to designation, and we have 75 district contact points plus HQ.
6. **The district alias problem is a genuine engineering finding.** 8 of 75 districts carry legacy names in their official contact addresses.
7. **A second scheme track exists inside the same agency**, with different eligibility and no income cap, and nothing tells a citizen which one fits them. That is our problem statement, demonstrated inside a single government body.
